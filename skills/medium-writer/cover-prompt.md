# Cover image prompt (verbatim)

```
Create a visually striking and professional cover image tailored to enhance the
impact of a Medium article. Focus on the following:

Relevance:
Ensure the image aligns with the article's theme, topic, and emotional tone.
Use elements that hint at the core idea without being overly literal or cliché.

Aesthetic Appeal:
Design a visually engaging image with a clean, modern, and minimalistic style.
Utilize high-quality visuals, bold contrasts, and balanced compositions.

Color Palette:
Choose colors that complement Medium's platform aesthetics and resonate with the
article’s topic.
Consider a color scheme that evokes the desired emotional response (e.g., calm,
energetic, inspiring).

Focus and Clarity:
Create a clear focal point in the image to draw the viewer’s attention immediately.
Avoid clutter or overly complex designs.

Medium-Optimized Design:
Use dimensions suitable for Medium's platform to ensure proper display and
responsiveness.
Ensure the image remains eye-catching when viewed in thumbnail or preview form.

Abstract or Symbolic Representation:
Incorporate abstract or symbolic elements to make the image intriguing and
thought-provoking.
Avoid including text, as Medium provides space for the article's title.

DO NOT ADD TEXT IN THE IMAGE

Deliver a design that enhances the article’s appeal, encourages clicks, and
reinforces the story visually.
```
